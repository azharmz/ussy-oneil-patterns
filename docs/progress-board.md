## P7 frozen summary

P7 normalizes detector outputs without altering native semantics:

- shared statuses: `RECOGNIZED`, `REJECTED`, `AMBIGUOUS`, `INCOMPLETE`, `NOT_EVALUABLE`;
- fault severity: `REJECT`, `AMBIGUITY`, `INFO`;
- provenance: `THEORY`, `RESEARCH`, `CONTEXT`;
- explicit adapters for Flat Base, Double Bottom, Cup body, Handle, Cup family, Ascending Base and Base-on-Base;
- native states/fault codes and source contract versions remain preserved;
- unrelated simultaneous recognized patterns yield `MULTI_PATTERN_AMBIGUITY`, not arbitrary winner selection;
- Cup body -> completed Cup family is an explicit hierarchy rather than a contradiction.

## Frozen data-consumption decisions

- upstream source of truth: `azharmz/ussy-data`;
- consume `production/ready/current.json`, never hardcode run parquet UUID;
- required fields: `date, security_id, ticker, open, high, low, close, adj_close, volume`;
- upstream Yahoo ingestion uses `auto_adjust=False`;
- raw OHLC and `adj_close` remain distinct;
- structural morphology/pivots use raw OHLC under current spec;
- historical evaluation receives only rows where `date <= asof_date`.

## P8 — Labelled morphology validation active objective

P8 is the first phase that must challenge the research-only numerical morphology translations against examples not generated by those same rules. Synthetic fixtures remain regression tests, not authoritative validation.

Research-only parameters requiring P8 evidence include at minimum:

- Flat Base tightness / wide-loose bands;
- Double Bottom clear-undercut and middle-rebound bands;
- Cup roundedness, bottom continuity, meaningful-depth and right-rim recovery bands;
- Ascending Base pullback-depth consistency;
- Base-on-Base numerical translation of “mostly above.”

## P8 anti-circularity rules

- synthetic fixtures created from detector assumptions cannot count as P8 labels;
- labels must include source/provenance and must be independent of trading returns;
- thresholds may be revised from morphology agreement only, never post-pattern stock performance;
- unclear examples remain explicitly ambiguous/disputed rather than forced into a class;
- training/tuning examples and final validation examples must be distinguishable if threshold revisions occur.

## Hard constraints

- no future bars or backdated confirmation;
- no trading-return-based detector tuning;
- no pattern family may silently reinterpret another detector's research-only band as a theory rule;
- no fabricated authoritative labels;
- no CAN SLIM eligibility, entry optimization, portfolio, or sell logic in #33.
